01 Profile

I have been making music for as long as I can remember. I wrote my first songs when I was eight and released my first track at eleven. Secondary school was the first time I got paid: I composed songs for a musical made together with the dental care services of Pori (a town in Finland), and also acted in it. That's how I got started with music and media in general.

I am first and foremost an artist. After years of releasing music myself, working together with many different labels and contributors, I learned a thing or two about how some things are done in the industry, and other artists started coming up to me with questions. That is how I ended up doing A&R work without really planning it. It's also a kind of unwritten rule in the music scene: I got a lot of help from older artists when I started, and now it is my turn to pass it on.

In 2026 I started studying ICT at Keuda to widen my toolbox. I like solving real problems, and my latest project is a tool that artists now use for their actual releases.

03 Projects

Julkaisuapuri – release helper for artists

2026

Releasing a song with a label means a lot of things to remember. Something gets forgotten or goes wrong, and then the emails start coming back and forth. Everyone loses time. I built a step-by-step checklist that follows the label's release instructions and even writes the message to the contact person, so it goes right the first time. Artists have given good feedback on how much clearer the process became.
